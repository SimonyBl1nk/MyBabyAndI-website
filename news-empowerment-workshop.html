Event Recap 3 October 2026

Women's Empowerment Workshop — Identity, Motherhood & Wellbeing

Our inaugural empowerment workshop brought together mothers from across Renfrewshire for an inspiring session on identity, motherhood and wellbeing — the first in a year-long series running through to August 2027.

On the day

On Saturday 3 October, women from across Glasgow and Renfrewshire gathered at the Wellness Suite, Lagoon Leisure Centre, Paisley, for our very first Women's Empowerment Workshop. Facilitated by our founder and two inspiring guest speakers, the session created a warm and honest space for mothers to explore who they are beyond the role of "mum" — their identity, their wellbeing, and the strength they carry every day.

Free childminding was provided so that no mother had to choose between attending and caring for her children. The turnout was wonderful, and the energy in the room was something we will not forget.

What we explored

  • Identity — who are you beyond being a mother?
  • Motherhood — sharing experiences across cultures and backgrounds
  • Wellbeing — practical tools for looking after yourself
  • Community — building sisterhood and peer support
  • Goal setting — looking ahead with confidence

This workshop is the first in a year-long series of workshops and webinars running through to August 2027, supported by the National Lottery Community Fund and OneRen. Each session is completely free to attend.

Why this matters

For many of the women in our community, the pressures of motherhood — combined with the experience of settling in Scotland and navigating a new culture — can leave little room for self-reflection or personal growth. This workshop gave our members dedicated time and space to reconnect with themselves, in the company of women who truly understand.

"It felt so good to just be seen as a woman, not just a mum. I left feeling stronger and more connected to this community."

— Attendee, Women's Empowerment Workshop, October 2026
