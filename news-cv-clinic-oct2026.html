CV Clinic & Civil Service Application Support

A free, face-to-face session helping ethnic minority mothers build stronger CVs and navigate Civil Service applications — at One Ren, Paisley, on Friday 31 October, 11am–2pm.

Event Details

Friday 31 October 2026 11:00am – 2:00pm
One Ren, 11 Christie St, Paisley PA1 1NB Renfrewshire
Free to attend Supported by OneRen and the National Lottery Community Fund

About this session

Following the success of our May 2026 CV Clinic, we're back with another free, practical session designed to help ethnic minority mothers take confident steps into employment. This session focuses on CV writing and Civil Service application support — two areas where the right guidance can make all the difference.

Whether you are looking for your first job in Scotland, returning to work after having children, or ready for a new challenge, this session is for you.

What's covered

  • Building and strengthening your CV for UK employers
  • Understanding and completing Civil Service application forms
  • Writing competency-based personal statements
  • One-to-one CV review and feedback
  • Tips for interviews and building confidence
